## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is a front page: masthead "THE WORLD IN ONE IMAGE", dated Monday 23 November 2026. Lead story: the tanker war in the Strait of Hormuz escalates — two more tankers hit mines on Sunday, a sixth claimed struck. Also inside: the Russian corvette Merkury sunk off Benghazi, COP31 collapsing in Antalya, a Russian drone flattening a block of flats near Kyiv, and the Democrats' sweep of Congress.

## How it is built

`tools/render.py` draws the page with PIL and the DejaVu fonts. `main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
